             Get started with Medicare 

       
                         
            
             Medicare is health insurance for people 65 or older who meet citizenship or residency requirements. You may be eligible to get Medicare earlier if you have a disability, End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD), or ALS (also called Lou Gehrig’s disease).  Some people get Medicare automatically, others have to actively sign up -- it depends if you start getting retirement or disability benefits from Social Security before you turn 65.
